# Research Notes (Phase 1)

_Last updated: 2026-03-11_

## Method

Collected publicly available issuer/program rule references and conservative personal finance guidance. Notes here are descriptive, not legal/financial advice.

## Issuer and program rule notes

1. **Chase 5/24 framing** (widely documented in points community references): applicants are often denied if they opened 5+ personal cards (across issuers) in the last 24 months.
   - Source: NerdWallet explainer on Chase 5/24 and card approval patterns (accessed 2026-03-11): https://www.nerdwallet.com/article/travel/chase-5-24-rule-explained
   - Source: The Points Guy overview (accessed 2026-03-11): https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase-5-24-rule/

2. **American Express welcome-offer eligibility language** commonly includes “may not be eligible for welcome offer” and lifetime-family restrictions.
   - Source: AmEx offer terms examples (public offer pages, accessed 2026-03-11): https://www.americanexpress.com/
   - Source summary: Frequent Miler AmEx application rules overview (accessed 2026-03-11): https://frequentmiler.com/amex-application-rules/

3. **Citi family language and bonus timing**: many products have language restricting bonuses if received/opened/closed similar family cards within specified windows.
   - Source: Citi card offer terms pages (accessed 2026-03-11): https://www.citi.com/credit-cards
   - Secondary explainer: Doctor of Credit Citi rules roundup (accessed 2026-03-11): https://www.doctorofcredit.com/credit-card-reference-pages/#Citi

4. **Capital One velocity sensitivity**: approvals can be limited by recent applications and internal risk factors.
   - Source: Capital One product terms pages (accessed 2026-03-11): https://www.capitalone.com/credit-cards/
   - Secondary explainer: NerdWallet Capital One card approval guidance (accessed 2026-03-11).

## Sign-up bonus and annual fee pattern notes

- Sign-up bonuses typically require spend in a fixed window (commonly 3–6 months).
- Annual fees frequently post around first anniversary; downgrade/retention/close decisions should be reviewed before or near fee posting.
- Statement close + autopay + manual verification should be tracked as repeat operations.

## Credit-score and risk guardrail references

- **MyFICO** and issuer educational pages indicate hard inquiries and utilization affect score trajectory.
  - Source: myFICO score factors (accessed 2026-03-11): https://www.myfico.com/credit-education/whats-in-your-credit-score
- **CFPB** credit card guidance emphasizes on-time payment and full understanding of fees/APR.
  - Source: CFPB credit card basics (accessed 2026-03-11): https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/credit-cards/

## Conservative best-practice implications

- Avoid carry balances; pay in full monthly.
- No manufactured spending or purchasing spend equivalents.
- Avoid stacking multiple new-account events too quickly.
- Model rewards as cash-equivalent value with haircut assumptions to reduce optimism bias.
