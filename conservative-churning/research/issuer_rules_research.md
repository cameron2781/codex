# Conservative Churning Research Notes (Phase 1)

Date captured: 2026-03-11

## Scope
Research-only pass focused on issuer velocity rules, SUB terms, annual-fee patterns, credit-score guardrails, and conservative practices.

## Source notes

1. **Chase 5/24**
   - Source: Doctor of Credit explainer for Chase 5/24.
   - Summary: Chase generally declines when five or more personal cards were opened in the past 24 months.
   - Reliability note: Third-party summary; validate against offer terms before applying.

2. **American Express welcome-bonus language**
   - Source: American Express card application terms pages.
   - Summary: Terms often include once-per-lifetime or family language for welcome offers.
   - Conservative interpretation: treat prior ownership as likely ineligible unless explicit pop-up approval confirms bonus.

3. **Citi family restriction**
   - Source: Citi card offer terms pages and product comparison pages.
   - Summary: Many bonuses use a 48-month family rule from prior bonus or account closure/opening events.
   - Conservative interpretation: block recommendations inside 48 months without manual term re-check.

4. **Capital One application velocity**
   - Source: The Points Guy issuer rules roundup.
   - Summary: Frequent reports of tighter approvals with rapid applications.
   - Conservative interpretation: prefer one new Capital One personal card every 6 months.

5. **Credit utilization and score guardrails**
   - Source: CFPB and Experian educational guidance on utilization and on-time payments.
   - Summary: On-time payment history and low utilization are core credit-score drivers.
   - Conservative interpretation: keep utilization low, no carried balances, and avoid clustered hard pulls.

6. **Annual fee review timing**
   - Source: issuer account terms and common retention practice guidance.
   - Summary: annual fee posts around anniversary; downgrade/cancel windows are often near fee posting.
   - Conservative interpretation: create review task immediately at fee posting and resolve within ~30 days.

- Citi ThankYou Points: **$0.01/point**.

No transfer-partner uplift assumptions in v1.
